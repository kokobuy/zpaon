05 / Answer center

Questions, answered.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

It is a curated collection of shopping links, categories and sometimes product notes. It simplifies discovery but does not independently certify the sellers or products listed.

Is the Kakobuy Spreadsheet 2026 free?

Public spreadsheet browsing is generally free. Purchasing products, using shopping services and arranging international delivery may involve separate charges.

How do I use Kakobuy spreadsheet links?

Select a category, open a listing and verify the seller, item specifications, options and current price. Review applicable policies before ordering.

How can I check Kakobuy QC photos?

Inspect multiple angles, visible defects, stitching, measurements and labels. Ask for additional images when details are missing. Photographs cannot prove every aspect of product quality.

How much does Kakobuy shipping cost?

Costs depend on destination, parcel weight, dimensions, carrier and optional services. Customs duties and taxes may be additional. Obtain a current quote for your particular shipment.

Does a Kakobuy spreadsheet guarantee authenticity?

No. An index does not authenticate products or establish authorization to sell branded merchandise. Verify product claims and respect intellectual property and import laws.

Can US and European buyers use these links?

They can browse the external index, but purchase eligibility, shipping availability, product restrictions and import obligations depend on the destination and the provider.
